Theme

Sea of Voices Each year, TEDxGunn High School Youth aims to center its annual conference's content around a central theme. Previous themes have included Crossroads, New Perspectives, One Step Forward, Redefining Normal, Call to Adventure, and Blossom.

Sea of Voices is devoted to those intrepid speakers who refuse to take life at face value, instead seeking deeper meaning and more profound understanding. With our distinguished speakers and performers, we hope to elevate the ordinary and re-analyse the everyday, bringing new meaning to life.
